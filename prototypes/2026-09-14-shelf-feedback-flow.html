Customize Your Shelf

A Gutbuster take on YouTube's "not interested" loop: how a viewer tells the shelf what to stop showing them, right from the card, with a back room to review the damage.

Reference: YouTube web, "Hiding a video" & history-management flows, via Mobbin. Rebuilt in Gutbuster's rental-shelf logic below, six steps, no lifted UI.

Step 1 / 6

The shelf, as usual

The Harold row on the homepage. Every spine carries a small dots button in the corner, same as any other card. Nothing announces "customize me." It shouldn't have to.

Vs. YouTubeThe kebab menu on a thumbnail becomes a dots button on a spine, same corner, same job.

Step 6 / 6

The back room

Every skip lands here too, under Account. Nothing is gone for good until you say so twice: once at the shelf, once in the file box. Put it back and it's eligible again next restock.

Vs. YouTubeTheir Watch History / Manage-all-history screen, rebuilt as a plain list of what you've skipped instead of everything you've watched.

Six steps, one clerk's logic

Every control names the thing it does and keeps that name through the flow: Skip, Undo, File It, Put It Back. No arrows on buttons, no dashes in the copy, and the reshuffle always gets a line of text instead of happening silently.
