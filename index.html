How the store works

No membership card. No drop box. Three steps, and the third one is optional.

  1. Walk an aisle

    Improv, standup or sketch. Then a shelf: a form, a team, a theater.

  2. Pull a tape

    Full sets for a night in. Scene clips for a coffee break. Every case says which.

  3. Follow the team

    Liked the Harold? The team has more on the shelf, and so does their home theater.
